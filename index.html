Frequently Asked Questions

Is QuintAI required for the use of Quintessentia Network Inc. products?

No, but QuintAI may be purchased to enhance your experience. It is not required for the use of our other products or even for the use of our other smart capabilities.

What AI model do you use for QuintAI?

QuintAI is powered by OpenAI’s solutions. If you choose to use QuintAI, you agree to OpenAI being a subprocessor of your data. OpenAI is located in the United States.

Will my data be used to train other AI models?

Your data won’t be used to train the AI models for the purpose of other Quintessentia Network Inc. Customers. We do not permit our third-party Subprocessors of QuintAI to use your data to train their Al models.
